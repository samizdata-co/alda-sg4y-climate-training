# Digital tools and good practices for youth–local authority cooperation on climate (Black Sea Basin) and SG4Y-CLIMATE project context

Research date: 3 November 2026. Searches in EN/RO/TR/BG/UA. Every claim has a source; anything not confirmed is listed under Gaps.

## 1. SG4Y-CLIMATE project: funding, partners, cities, aims, timeline, TYCCA, Digital Guideline

### Takeaway
The project is confirmed: SG4Y-CLIMATE ("Smart Governance for Youth Involvement in Climate Action") is project BSB01316, funded by Interreg NEXT Black Sea Basin. It runs 4 Nov 2025 – 3 May 2027 with 4 partners: Istanbul Nişantaşı University (lead), ALDA Moldova, Galați Municipality and Tekirdağ Metropolitan Municipality. TYCCA = Transnational Youth Council for Climate Action. Two of the brief's assumptions are off. Chișinău City Hall is **not** a partner: Moldova is represented by ALDA Moldova, which is based in Chișinău. And no public source mentions a "Digital Guideline" due in December 2026.

### Cited Findings
- Official programme record: code **BSB01316**, acronym SG4Y-CLIMATE, "Smart Governance for Youth Involvement in Climate Action". Countries: Republic of Moldova, Romania, Türkiye. Call: "Second call for SMALL SCALE projects". Priority "03 – Competent and Resilient Region". Specific objective "ISO6.3 – Building up mutual trust, in particular by encouraging people-to-people actions". Start 04 November 2025, end 03 May 2027, status ongoing. — [Interreg NEXT BSB project page](https://blacksea-cbc.net/interreg-next-bsb-2021-2027/projects/bsb01316)
- Total budget €548,716.33, of which Interreg funds €493,844.69. The programme's indicators list "4 organizations cooperating across borders", "1 jointly developed solution" and "39 participants in the joint actions". — [Interreg NEXT BSB project page](https://blacksea-cbc.net/interreg-next-bsb-2021-2027/projects/bsb01316)
- The programme summary describes the problem as rising temperatures, water scarcity, flooding and biodiversity loss in the Black Sea Basin, with youth "largely excluded from environmental decision-making". It says the project responds with "a smart governance methodology, AI-enabled digital participation platforms, and a Transnational Youth Council for Climate Action". — [Interreg NEXT BSB project page](https://blacksea-cbc.net/interreg-next-bsb-2021-2027/projects/bsb01316)
- The project website lists Istanbul Nişantaşı University as Lead Partner (Türkiye), plus ALDA Moldova, Galati Municipality (Romania) and Tekirdağ Metropolitan Municipality (Türkiye). It gives the same budget (€548,716.33) and an 18-month duration. — [smartgovernance4youth.org](https://smartgovernance4youth.org/en)
- The website's 7 work packages: (1) Management Setup; (2) Transnational Youth Council for Climate Action; (3) Developing the Smart Governance Methodology; (4) Development of Digital Platform; (5) AI Integration to the Digital Platform; (6) Transnational Awareness Raising Campaign; (7) Capacity Building Activities for Municipalities. No dates are given. — [Project page](https://smartgovernance4youth.org/en/project)
- The 4 stated objectives: increase youth participation in climate governance; strengthen local authorities' participatory decision-making; create AI-supported digital governance tools; build transnational cooperation across the Black Sea Basin. — [Project page](https://smartgovernance4youth.org/en/project)
- ALDA's announcement names the partners as Istanbul Nisantasi University (lead), Tekirdag Metropolitan Municipality, Galati Municipality and **ALDA Moldova, the Chișinău branch of ALDA**. Outputs: TYCCA, a Smart Governance Methodology "supporting municipalities in designing participatory processes that include youth", an "AI-based digital platform" for ongoing consultation and co-creation, and a Transnational Climate Resilience Campaign. ALDA Moldova coordinates youth participation, communication and transnational campaigning. — [ALDA project news](https://www.alda-europe.eu/?p=80850); see also [ALDA SG4Y page](https://www.alda-europe.eu/sg4y-climate-youth-action/)
- Kick-off and launch conference: Istanbul, 5–7 March 2026, with local and central authorities, academia, civil society and youth. ALDA will deliver "webinars and events" on the Smart Governance Methodology and AI-based digital tools. The conference covered AI-supported data analysis for evidence-based decisions. — [ALDA kick-off article, 11 Mar 2026](https://www.alda-europe.eu/sg4y-climate-kick-off-meeting-istanbul-youth-climate-action/)
- **TYCCA = Transnational Youth Council for Climate Action.** It is now operational with 35 youth (4 team leaders + 31 members) and 4 mentors from Türkiye, Romania and Moldova, in 4 working groups: WG1 Health Impact (8 youth + 1 mentor); WG2 Law and Policy (7 + 1); WG3 Innovation and Solutions (8 + 1); WG4 Participation in Decision Making (12 + 1). — [TYCCA page](https://smartgovernance4youth.org/en/tycca)
- TYCCA call (Tekirdağ Metropolitan Municipality) was open to ages 18–29 with sufficient English, interested in climate, sustainability and youth participation. Selected members attend international events in Moldova and Türkiye, receive training and join awareness campaigns. The call also confirms the project period as "November 2025 and May 2027". **Deadline discrepancy:** the English text says 15 April 2026; the Turkish text says 10 May 2026. — [Tekirdağ BB TYCCA call PDF](https://www.tekirdag.bel.tr/content/WebSource/file/duyuru/1777972029_tycca_lan_metni.pdf)
- Search engine summaries mentioned a "total budget of €644,204" on an ALDA-linked page, but the page was ambiguous about which project it referred to. The official programme figure is €548,716.33. — [ALDA search result](https://www.alda-europe.eu/?p=80850) vs [Interreg record](https://blacksea-cbc.net/interreg-next-bsb-2021-2027/projects/bsb01316)

### Inferences
- "INU" in the brief most likely stands for Istanbul Nişantaşı University (the lead partner), not an Italian or other body.
- In the handout, "partner cities" should read **Galați (RO) and Tekirdağ (TR)** as municipal partners, with Chișinău (MD) present through ALDA Moldova, plus Istanbul through the lead university. Calling Chișinău City Hall a partner would be inaccurate.
- The "1 jointly developed solution" indicator presumably refers to the AI-enabled digital platform, possibly packaged with the methodology. A "Digital Guideline" may be an internal deliverable name, perhaps tied to WP3 or WP4, that does not appear in public materials.

### Gaps
- **Digital Guideline (Dec 2026):** not mentioned on the project website (Project, TYCCA pages), the ALDA pages or the Interreg record. It is unverified and should be confirmed with ALDA Moldova or the project's internal workplan.
- The project's own pages give no milestone dates for the platform, AI integration, methodology or campaign. The six "Project file" PDFs on the Interreg page (P1–P6) were not read.
- TYCCA selection procedure and launch date are not published on the TYCCA page.
- The digital platform's URL and its status (live or in development) were not found.

## 2. Digital participation platforms used by municipalities in the region

### Takeaway
Open-source participatory budgeting platforms are well established in the wider region. Sofia runs Decidim ("Sofia Decides", 11,589 voters in its first round). Brașov, Sinaia and Dumbrăvița run CONSUL, deployed in Romania via Code for Romania. Ukraine's e-DEM serves 200+ communities. Chișinău's civic budget (3 million lei/year) appears to work mainly through paper and document applications. No dedicated digital PB or reporting app was found for Galați or Tekirdağ.

### Cited Findings
**Moldova**
- Chișinău "Bugetul Civic 2026": 3 million lei per year; small projects up to 100,000 lei, large up to 300,000 lei. Applicants submit a request, proof of co-financing (1% for individuals, 20% for legal entities) and supporting signatures by 28 Feb 2026. Eligible themes include public-space improvement, urban mobility, innovation and energy efficiency. The article mentions no online voting platform. — [Logos Press, 3 Feb 2026](https://logos-pres.md/noutati/locuitorii-capitalei-au-primit-3-milioane-de-lei-pentru-proiectele-lor/)
- Context: Chișinău ran on a provisional budget in 2025 after repeated failed council votes. — [Logos Press](https://logos-pres.md/noutati/a-noua-incercare-de-a-aproba-bugetul-capitalei-pentru-2025-esueaza/); [IPN](https://ipn.md/en/?p=63757)
- Moldova's national public consultation portal is particip.gov.md, where draft decisions and annexes are published. — [particip.gov.md](https://particip.gov.md/index.php/ro/widget_src?page=793)
- Smaller Moldovan local authorities are adopting PB regulations, e.g. Nisporeni (decision annex, March 2026). — [Primăria Nisporeni PB regulation](https://primarianisporeni.md/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Anexa-DECIZIE-6-14-regulament-bugetarea-participativa.pdf)

**Romania**
- Code for Romania (now part of Commit Global) built "Bugetare Participativă" for city halls, reusing Madrid's open-source CONSUL software and implemented free of charge. Brașov was the first city. — [StartupCafe](https://startupcafe.ro/propuneri-de-proiecte-bani-buget-local-dezvoltare-oras-platforma-it-isti-17445)
- The CONSUL Democracy fork list includes Romanian deployments in Brașov, Sinaia and Dumbrăvița. — [CONSUL Democracy, Jan 2025](https://consuldemocracy.org/2025/01/open-source-forks-and-modifications-of-consul-democracy-software/); Brașov case study listed at [docs.consuldemocracy.org](https://docs.consuldemocracy.org/use_cases/romania/brasov) (page returned an error when fetched)
- PB uptake remains limited: in 2023 only 14 county-seat cities and one Bucharest sector ran PB programmes, with modest budgets. — [HotNews](https://hotnews.ro/?p=17078)
- Bucharest Sector 2 cancelled a tender for an online project-submission platform. — [Profit.ro](https://profit.ro/stiri/implicarea-cetatenilor-sectorului-2-in-cheltuirea-banilor-mai-asteapta-20444900)
- Commit Global was launched in The Hague in September 2023 as the international arm of Code for Romania. It reports 70+ "tech for good" solutions and has a "Climate infrastructure" pillar, though that page names no specific climate products. — [Romania Insider](https://romania-insider.com/code-romania-becomes-global-organization-2023); [Commit Global climate page](https://www.commitglobal.org/en/climate-infrastructure); [Commit Global GitHub](https://www.github.com/commitglobal)
- Galați: no dedicated PB or reporting app was found. Petitions arrive by email (sesizari@primariagalati.ro), according to the mayor's 2018 report. Galați County Council publishes half-yearly petition reports (2025, 2026). — [Raport primar 2018](https://www.primariagalati.ro/portal/galati/portal.nsf/AllByUNID/945FBB6AC1118494C225863B002D5E55/$FILE/raport_primar2018.pdf); [CJ Galați petition report H1 2026](https://cjgalati.ro/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Raport-petitii-semestrul-I-2026.pdf)
- Galați youth precedents: "Elevii, aleși locali pentru o zi" (pupils sat in the local council and voted on draft decisions; date not shown). Separately, two Galați pupils won a municipality–civil society competition with an app for reporting abuse (2022). — [Adevărul](https://adevarul.ro/stiri-locale/galati/elevii-alesi-locali-pentru-o-zi-la-galati-cine-a-2172154.html); [Antena3](https://www.antena3.ro/emisiuni/romania-inteligenta/aplicatie-victime-abuzuri-creata-elevi-galati-635935.html)
- Cautionary example: Timișoara's mobile app for citizen reports went largely unused. — [Digi24](https://www.digi24.ro/regional/digi24-timisoara/aplicatie-mobila-pentru-sesizari-neutilizata-1060896)

**Bulgaria**
- "Sofia Decides" (Reshava Sofia, https://reshava.sofia.bg/) runs on Decidim and is the first platform in Bulgaria to integrate Decidim with national e-Authentication. Its first Citizens' Budget round (2024) produced 450+ proposals, of which 250+ went to a vote. **11,589 residents voted (7,105 online, 4,484 in person).** Sofia allocated BGN 5.3 million (~€2.71m), split into BGN 500,000 for city-wide projects and BGN 200,000 for each of 24 districts. Projects must be completed within 15 months. "Greening" is a project category; selected projects include bicycle paths, playgrounds and street lighting. Platform set-up cost was €25,000–50,000, and transfer to Varna is planned. Voting is 18+ only. — [Interreg Europe good practice (published 30 Sep 2026)](https://www.interregeurope.eu/good-practices/sofia-decides-civic-participation-platform-for-transparent-local-decision-making)

**Türkiye**
- "Beyaz Masa" (White Desk) request and complaint apps exist for several municipalities, including İBB Istanbul and Kayseri. — [İBB BeyazMasa app listing](https://www.tamindir.com/indir/ibb-beyazmasa/); [Akıllı Şehir Kayseri](https://apps.apple.com/tr/app/ak%C4%B1ll%C4%B1-%C5%9Fehir-kayseri/id1272195374)
- Youth-specific municipal apps exist, e.g. "Genç Gaziantep". — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1592442722)
- Tekirdağ youth participation: "Young Voices, Participatory Cities", run by STGM with GSM and the Turkey Europe Foundation. It was a 9-day in-person training for 20 Tekirdağ residents aged 18–35 (applications closed 24 Oct 2025). Participants did needs analysis, city walks and mapping in Süleymanpaşa, then presented proposals at 5 dialogue meetings with local government representatives. No digital tools or climate focus were mentioned. — [STGM](https://www.stgm.org.tr/en/young-voices-shaping-their-cities-tekirdag-applications-open-young-voices-participatory-cities)

**Armenia / Ukraine**
- Yerevan: a 2019–2020 PB pilot used online idea collection and voting. It received 740 proposals; about 1% of eligible voters took part, and 17 projects worth US$1m were selected. Yerevan's OGP Action Plan 2025–2028 commits to a PB module on the "Active Citizen" platform; the commitment notes that no structured digital PB currently exists. Yerevan also runs a "Green Development" platform with real-time air quality monitoring. — [OGP commitment AMERV0008](https://www.opengovpartnership.org/members/yerevan-armenia/commitments/AMERV0008/); [Yerevan Action Plan 2025–2028](https://www.opengovpartnership.org/documents/action-plan-yerevan-armenia-2025-2028/); [Armenpress on Active Citizen](https://armenpress.am/eng/amp/981700)
- Armenia national PB: since March 2024 several communities (Jermuk, Abovyan, Ashtarak, Armavir) accept proposals and votes online. — [ARKA](https://arka.am/en/news/society/program_of_joint_budgeting_of_projects_proposed_by_community_residents_launched_in_armenia_/)
- Ukraine: e-DEM is the national e-democracy platform for petitions, consultations and PB, launched in 2015 as a local e-petition system. A Ministry of Regional Development item says the municipal budget tool runs in 200 municipalities and has been used by over 1 million Ukrainians. — [OGP UA0093](https://opengovpartnership.org/members/ukraine/commitments/UA0093); [Decentralization.ua](https://www.decentralization.ua/en/news/14353); [Civic Tech Guide – Hromadskyi Project](https://civictech.guide/projects/hromadski-project)

### Inferences
- Sofia Decides is the strongest recent, measurable, nearby benchmark for a digital PB with a greening category. Its lesson for youth is that the 18+ eligibility excludes 16–17-year-olds. A youth-specific stream would need a separate rule.
- For no-code youth groups, the practical entry points are using existing municipal channels: submitting to the Chișinău civic budget, filing e-petitions or reports, and responding on particip.gov.md or consultare.gov.ro. Deploying Decidim or CONSUL is not a realistic entry point, since both need developers (Decidim is Ruby on Rails — [IADB code index](https://code.iadb.org/en/tools/decidim)).

### Gaps
- No evidence found of Decidim or CONSUL in Moldova, Georgia or Türkiye municipalities.
- No current Galați PB programme or Tekirdağ Beyaz Masa app was confirmed. Check primariagalati.ro and tekirdag.bel.tr directly.
- A "Primăria Mea"-style municipal app for Chișinău was not verified (a Substack called "primariamea24" exists, but it is a newsletter, not a city app).
- Commit Global's current named product list (Monitorizare Vot, Rezultate Vot, etc.) could not be retrieved from its site (the solutions page returned 404). "Monitorizare Vot" is confirmed by press coverage only. "Civic Labs" and "Monitor Watch" were not verified.

## 3. Youth-focused climate digital engagement examples (citizen science, mapping, youth councils, Fridays for Future, Climate Pact)

### Takeaway
Low-cost air-quality sensor networks (Aerlive in Romania and Moldova, PurpleAir in Cluj) and student field mapping in Bucharest are the most transferable, no-coding citizen-science models found. Cluj's Com'ON youth PB (with a Green Mission 2030 strand) is the best youth–municipality cooperation example in the region. Fridays for Future activity in MD, RO and TR appears dormant since around 2020.

### Cited Findings
- **Aer Live Moldova**: a civic network monitoring Chișinău air quality with its own sensors, with data published on an open online platform. It reported 10 sensors in Chișinău, 6 private sensors not yet connected, sensors in Rezina, and planned expansion to Bălți. It ran a campaign urging citizens and businesses to buy and host sensors. The article is undated. — [Radio Moldova](https://radiomoldova.md/p/84764)
- **Cluj "Ce aer respir"**: NGOs (Romanian Health Observatory, Clujul Sustenabil, Fundația Comunitară Cluj) crowdfunded 22 PurpleAir PA-II PM2.5/PM10 sensors. City Hall declined to host them on InfoTrafic poles, so the organisers asked residents and businesses to "adopt" sensors. Data go to Aerlive.ro (Ecopolis and Fundația ETA2U). This is a 2021 example of authority non-cooperation and a civic workaround. — [Actual de Cluj, 12 Apr 2021](https://actualdecluj.ro/refuzati-de-primarie-civicii-din-cluj-cheama-comunitatea-sa-adopte-cei-22-de-senzorii-de-monitorizare-a-aerului-cumparati-in-urma-unei-campanii-de-strangere-de-fonduri/)
- Official Chișinău context: a UNDP project announced air-quality sensors in 5 city sectors with real-time web data (2019; completion not confirmed). — [UNDP](https://www.undp.org/node/103431)
- **Bucharest student heat and tree mapping**: 15 Environmental Science students mapped 135 Bucharest streets in summer (2022 and 2025) to record heat-stressed and drying trees. The work is presented as a scalable citizen-science model for urban planning. — [ECSA 2026 conference abstract](https://test2.nomadit.co.uk/conference/ecsa2026/paper/92682) (page later returned 404; content from search summary only, so treat as partially verified)
- Bucharest heat perception survey (EGU25): 41% of respondents did not know what "urban heat island" means. — [EGU25-16862](https://meetingorganizer.copernicus.org/EGU25/EGU25-16862.html?pdf)
- Model from outside the region: Barcelona's "Cròniques de la calor" uses citizen participation to identify public spaces worst affected by extreme heat. — [Universitat de Barcelona](https://web.ub.edu/en/web/actualitat/w/heat-chronicles?tn=np)
- **Com'ON Cluj-Napoca** (youth PB since 2015, run by Grupul PONT with Federația Tinerilor din Cluj, Cluj for Youth, and Cluj-Napoca City Hall and Local Council). Com'ON Green Mission 2030 (Dec 2024–Apr 2025) supports young people's ideas linked to Cluj's 2030 climate-neutrality goal through mentoring and prizes. It won 1st place, "Verde pentru Viitor", at the Romanian Youth Gala (25 Oct 2025). Com'ON School won 3rd, "Participare Democratică". The programme holds a URBACT Good Practice label and is funded by Fondation Botnar and BCR. — [Actual de Cluj](https://actualdecluj.ro/programul-de-bugetare-participativa-pentru-tineri-botezat-comon-cluj-napoca-dublu-premiat-la-gala-tineretului-din-romania-2025/)
- **LCOY Türkiye 2025** (Local Conference of Youth) was hosted by Konya Metropolitan Municipality with support from the Ministry of Environment, Urbanisation and Climate Change and UNICEF, with youth "Climate Ambassadors" attending. — [Ekonomim](https://www.ekonomim.com/sehirler/iklim-elcilerinin-katildigi-lcoy2025-konya-buyuksehirin-ev-sahipliginde-basladi-haberi-845554)
- **European Climate Pact (Romania)**: 40 Romanian ambassadors as of April 2025, with the national secretariat run by REPER21. Several ambassadors run youth activities: climate games for rural children (Laura Soroceanu), student climate clubs (Andrada Istrate-Luca), monthly school events (Andreea-Mihaela Bălașa). — [Revista Biz](https://www.revistabiz.ro/romania-si-a-consolidat-echipa-de-ambasadori-ai-pactului-climatic-european/); [Climate Pact – Laura Soroceanu](https://climate-pact.europa.eu/meet-community/climate-pact-ambassadors/laura-soroceanu_mt); [Andrada Istrate-Luca](https://climate-pact.europa.eu/meet-community/climate-pact-ambassadors/andrada-istrate-luca_fr)
- **Fridays for Future**: the most visible Romanian action found was a 2020 protest at the Ministry of Environment, where activists left pairs of shoes blocking the entrance. Turkish FFF (Istanbul, Ankara) coverage dates from around 2019. No Moldovan FFF chapter was found. — [Energynomics](https://energynomics.ro/en/?p=140965); [Bianet](https://bianet.org/yazi/turkiye-de-iklim-grevi-ankara-hedefimiz-bir-gelecek-var-edebilmek-213157); [Hürriyet Daily News](https://hurriyetdailynews.com/turkish-students-to-join-global-climate-movement-146702)
- Moldova youth policy events: Moldova Youth Forum 2025 (EU Neighbours East). This is a general youth forum, not climate-specific. — [EU Neighbours East](https://euneighbourseast.eu/ro/opportunities/moldova-youth-forum-2025-o-zi-dedicata-dialogului-si-implicarii-tinerilor-din-moldova/)
- Ukraine youth: the Council of Europe trained 60 young Ukrainians in volunteerism and civic participation tools. — [CoE Kyiv office](https://www.coe.int/en/web/kyiv/-/youth-driving-community-change-60-ukrainians-mastered-volunteerism-and-civic-participation-tools)
- Reference guide: C40/NDC Partnership's "Partnering with youth: A city's guide to implementing youth climate councils". — [NDC Partnership](https://ndcpartnership.org/knowledge-portal/climate-toolbox/partnering-youth-citys-guide-implementing-youth-climate-councils)

### Inferences
- **Most transferable to small groups of young people with no coding skills:**
  1. Hosting or "adopting" a low-cost air sensor and using the public Aerlive maps as evidence (Aer Live Moldova and Aerlive.ro models).
  2. Street-level heat or tree-stress mapping walks with a phone and a shared spreadsheet or map (Bucharest student model; Barcelona "heat chronicles").
  3. Submitting a green project to an existing youth or civic PB (Com'ON Cluj; Chișinău Bugetul Civic; Sofia Decides).
  4. Needs analysis plus city walks plus dialogue meetings with local officials (STGM Tekirdağ model), with digital mapping added.
- Generic no-code tools commonly used for this kind of work (official sites, **not re-verified in this session**): OpenStreetMap / HOT Tasking Manager (openstreetmap.org, tasks.hotosm.org), KoboToolbox (kobotoolbox.org), Sensor.Community (sensor.community), iNaturalist (inaturalist.org), uMap (umap.openstreetmap.fr), Decidim (decidim.org) and CONSUL (consuldemocracy.org).

### Gaps
- No 2023–2026 "heat walk" campaign was found in Chișinău, Galați or Tekirdağ.
- No OpenStreetMap mapping party tied to climate in MD, RO or TR was verified (not searched in depth).
- No Moldovan Climate Pact ambassadors or Moldovan FFF activity were found.
- The Aer Live Moldova article has no date, so its sensor counts may be outdated.

## 4. Good practices with measurable outcomes (authority changed a decision because of youth evidence or digital engagement)

### Takeaway
Measurable outcomes of digital engagement exist (Sofia: 11,589 voters, BGN 5.3m allocated; Yerevan pilot: 740 proposals, US$1m; Ukraine: over 1 million users). However, no well-documented 2023–2026 case was found in these countries where a municipality reversed or changed a climate or environment decision specifically because of youth-generated evidence. The Cluj sensor case is a documented counter-example: the authority declined to cooperate.

### Cited Findings
- Sofia Decides: 11,589 voters (7,105 online), BGN 5.3m allocated, projects to be delivered within 15 months, including bicycle paths and greening. — [Interreg Europe](https://www.interregeurope.eu/good-practices/sofia-decides-civic-participation-platform-for-transparent-local-decision-making)
- Yerevan PB pilot: 740 online proposals, about 1% voter turnout, 17 projects funded with US$1m. — [OGP AMERV0008](https://www.opengovpartnership.org/members/yerevan-armenia/commitments/AMERV0008/)
- Com'ON Cluj: youth ideas linked to the city's 2030 climate-neutrality goal, implemented with City Hall and Local Council as partners; national award (2025) and URBACT Good Practice label. Number of projects and budget were not stated. — [Actual de Cluj](https://actualdecluj.ro/programul-de-bugetare-participativa-pentru-tineri-botezat-comon-cluj-napoca-dublu-premiat-la-gala-tineretului-din-romania-2025/)
- Cluj City Hall refused to host civic air sensors, citing its own integrated air-quality plan. — [Actual de Cluj, 2021](https://actualdecluj.ro/refuzati-de-primarie-civicii-din-cluj-cheama-comunitatea-sa-adopte-cei-22-de-senzorii-de-monitorizare-a-aerului-cumparati-in-urma-unei-campanii-de-strangere-de-fonduri/)
- Young activists, ecologists and NGOs held a silent protest against construction in Yerevan's Students' Park. The outcome was not shown in the source. — [Transparency International Anti-corruption Center](https://transparency.am/en/news/view/258)
- Romanian PB critique: publishing a PB regulation without complementary measures does not amount to real consultation. — [HotNews](https://hotnews.ro/despre-bugetarea-participativa-n-romnia-si-de-ce-este-ignorata-de-primari-91512)

### Inferences
- In the handout, present "decision changed because of youth evidence" as an aspiration and gap. That is itself an argument for SG4Y-CLIMATE's methodology. Use the PB examples as proof that digital channels convert into budget decisions.

### Gaps
- No verified example of a youth-evidence-driven decision reversal in MD, RO, TR, GE, AM, UA, BG or GR for 2023–2026. A search in English returned nothing relevant; local-language searches on specific cases (e.g. park or tree-felling disputes in Chișinău) would be needed.
- Climate-ADAPT and Covenant of Mayors good-practice databases were not searched within the tool budget.

## 5. Data journalism and open-data advocacy on climate (MD, RO, TR)

### Takeaway
Credible outlets exist: İklim Haber (Türkiye, data-driven, part of Covering Climate Now), RISE Moldova (OSINT and investigations, with a 2025 annual report), and Aerlive's open sensor data in RO and MD. No specific 2023–2026 climate data-journalism project by RISE Moldova, Recorder or Funky Citizens was verified.

### Cited Findings
- İklim Haber describes itself as an independent, data-based alternative climate media outlet and a member of Covering Climate Now. It says it aims to create political change by addressing decision-makers. — [Sivil Sayfalar interview](https://sivilsayfalar.org/?p=76324)
- T24's "İklim Masası" is a column and news service supplying scientist-written climate news to journalists free of charge. — [T24](https://t24.com.tr/yazarlar/iklim-masasi)

### Inferences
- For youth with no coding skills, the most practical "data advocacy" path is to combine public data (Aerlive maps, Hydrometeorological Service bulletins, Climate Action Tracker) with simple charts (e.g. Datawrapper or Flourish free tiers, not verified here). Then pitch the story to an outlet such as İklim Haber, or submit it through the municipal consultation channel.
